Key Insights
Data-driven findings from the readmission analysis
🚨 Hip Fracture Highest Risk
Hip fracture patients showed the highest 30-day readmission rate at 49.6%, nearly 12 points above the overall average.
👴 Age is the #1 Predictor
Patients 75+ have a 54.1% readmission rate — more than double the 22.1% rate for patients aged 18–39.
💊 Medications Signal Risk
Number of medications ranked 2nd in the model — likely a proxy for disease burden and polypharmacy complications.
🏥 AMA Discharges Dangerous
Patients who left Against Medical Advice had a 44.2% readmission rate vs 35.7% for standard home discharge.
💳 Uninsured 11pts Higher
Uninsured patients had a 44.6% readmission rate vs 33.7% for privately insured — a 11-point access-to-care gap.
📅 December Spike
December had the highest monthly readmission rate at 42.7%, suggesting staffing and holiday-period care gaps.
